============================================================
1. PRODUCT VISION
============================================================

YatraGuard is a centralized mobile platform for major pilgrimage and
religious-tourism destinations in India.

The platform serves TWO major purposes:

1. PILGRIM / TOURIST ASSISTANCE
2. CROWD MANAGEMENT AND STAMPEDE PREVENTION

The application should combine:

- pilgrimage information
- tourism information
- transportation
- accommodation
- crowd conditions
- waiting times
- safety information
- emergency services
- navigation
- alerts
- recommendations
- itinerary planning
- AI-powered guidance

into ONE centralized platform.

The central innovation is:

Instead of pilgrims independently searching Google, maps, hotels,
transportation, temple information, crowd conditions and safety
information across multiple applications, YatraGuard combines them into
a single platform.

The system should eventually help prevent dangerous crowd accumulation
by informing pilgrims BEFORE they enter an overcrowded area and
redirecting them toward safer alternatives.

IMPORTANT:

This is the FIRST MVP.

Use realistic MOCK / SAMPLE DATA.

DO NOT integrate real government systems, CCTV, IoT sensors,
real-time temple APIs, payment systems, or real crowd tracking yet.

However, design the architecture so those systems can be integrated later.

============================================================
2. CORE PROBLEM
============================================================

Major pilgrimage destinations can experience:

- overcrowding
- long queues
- congestion
- sudden crowd accumulation
- transportation bottlenecks
- parking congestion
- poor distribution of visitors
- lack of centralized information
- pilgrims entering already crowded areas
- lack of awareness about alternative nearby locations
- emergency response difficulties

The application should eventually solve this by providing:

"REAL-TIME SITUATIONAL AWARENESS + SMART GUIDANCE."

The core principle is:

DO NOT JUST TELL THE USER WHERE TO GO.

TELL THEM:

- where they can safely go
- where they should avoid
- why they should avoid it
- when it may be safer
- what alternative location they can visit
- how to reach that alternative

32. RECOMMENDATION ENGINE
============================================================

Create a simple rule-based engine for MVP.

Rules:

IF occupancy > 100%
THEN recommend avoiding the location.

IF occupancy > 110%
THEN mark CRITICAL.

IF occupancy > 120%
THEN mark DANGEROUS.

IF crowd increasing rapidly
THEN issue warning.

IF alternative location has significantly lower occupancy
THEN recommend alternative.

IF weather is bad
THEN prioritize indoor locations.

IF parking availability < 10%
THEN recommend alternative parking.

IF waiting time > 180 minutes
THEN recommend alternative attraction.

This will later be replaced/enhanced with Gemini/ML.
